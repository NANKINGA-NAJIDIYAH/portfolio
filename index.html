About Me
Purposeful engineering,
rooted in personal story

I am a final-year Biomedical Engineering student at Makerere University, Uganda, driven by a deeply personal mission. My younger brother was diagnosed with cerebral palsy at age two — an experience that shaped my understanding of healthcare gaps and strengthened my resolve to create solutions that matter.

My work spans maternal health, neonatal care, assistive technology, and AI in healthcare. I am committed to building devices and systems that work in real African clinical settings — not just in well-resourced labs.

Beyond engineering, I hold three leadership roles, am an Aspire Leaders Program alumna, and an AWS AI & ML Scholar. I aspire to pursue a Masters and PhD, establish an NGO focused on cerebral palsy, and ultimately contribute to science and technology policy at the national level.
